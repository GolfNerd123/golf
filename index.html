Score Guide
Below your name
Stroke play: "85 (+5)" — total strokes and how far from par. Negative is under par, positive is over.

Stableford: "12 pts" — your running Stableford points.

If you have a handicap: "[5 (+1)] · HCP 7" — the bracketed number is your net score (strokes after handicap is applied), with how far that is from par. HCP 7 is the number of strokes you get on this course.
In the circle
The big number is your strokes for this hole. Below it: E = par  ·  +1 = bogey  ·  −1 = birdie.
