How the Suggested Handicap Is Calculated
1. Score differential per round
For every completed round that has a course rating and slope rating, we compute:

(Gross score − Course Rating) × 113 ÷ Slope Rating

This is the World Handicap System (WHS) formula — it converts your raw score into a number that's comparable across different courses of different difficulty.
2. Most recent rounds
We take your most recent 20 qualifying rounds (fewer if you haven't played that many yet).
3. Best differentials only
Of those, we keep only your best 8 differentials (fewer if you have fewer than 8 rounds) — the same "best 8 of last 20" approach the real WHS uses, so a couple of bad rounds don't skew the number.
4. Average & adjustment
We average those best differentials, then multiply by 0.96 (the standard WHS bracket adjustment) and round to one decimal place. That's your suggested Handicap Index.
Róni — how it works
Teams
Two fixed 2-person teams play the whole round. All scoring uses net strokes (after handicap).
Every hole
Team point — the team with the lowest net ball (best of its two players) → +1 for both team members.

Individual point — the single lowest net score of all 4 players → +1 for that player.

Birdie point — any player with a net birdie → +1.

Eagle point — any player with a net eagle (or better) → +2.
Ties
If the team point or individual point is tied on a hole, nobody gets it that hole. Birdie and eagle points always count, no matter what anyone else scored.
Scoring
Team scores and individual scores are tracked separately — most points wins each.
